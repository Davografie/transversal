# Setting and Narrative

Transversal spans a multiverse of distinct universes, each operating under its own physical laws, accessible through razmerit — the hyper-dimensional material that bridges worlds.

## Earth

Earth is our world: mundane physics, no magic, no qi, no cultivation. It is also isolated — cut off from the greater multiversal neighborhood, unaware that other universes exist. The only exception is a single remnant: **the Jian of Still Waters**, an ancient Chinese-looking straight sword left behind by an immortal cultivator who arrived on Earth millennia ago and died here.

Earth's isolation is not an accident. Something — a forgotten event, a dead attunement, a deliberate sealing — cut Earth off from the rest of the multiverse. The jian is the last working bridge.

### Earth Factions

Earth is populated by real institutions. The game uses actual factions, locations, and characters where possible, grounded in verifiable reality.

| Faction | Role in Transversal |
|---------|---------------------|
| **Russian Federation — Artifact Research Directorate (ARD)** | Possesses the jian. A small, secretive division within the Russian scientific-military apparatus, tasked with studying anomalous artifacts recovered from expeditions. They know the jian is unusual. They don't know what it does. |
| **Dr. Mikhail Antonov** | Mathematician, specialist in multidimensional harmonics. Attached to the ARD as a consultant. The first human to understand what the jian actually is. |
| **Russian Intelligence (FSB)** | Maintains a presence in Krasnoyarsk-45 to monitor the ARD facility and its personnel. One agent in particular has noted Antonov's unusual behavior. |
| **The Players' Sponsor** | Whoever sent the players to extract Antonov. See [[#Player Group Origins]]. |

### Krasnoyarsk-45

A small, secluded town deep in Russian territory. Officially a closed administrative-territorial formation — one of Russia's "ZATO" towns, not marked on public maps. Its original purpose (a hydro-electric dam supporting a now-defunct research facility) was decommissioned decades ago. The dam still runs, powering the town's aging infrastructure.

The ARD facility is in the mountains above the town, accessible by a single road that's impassable in winter without a local guide. The town itself has:

- A small population of aging residents who remember the Soviet era
- One hotel, one bar, one general store
- An FSB agent posing as a municipal administrator
- Old Soviet infrastructure — functional but decaying

In winter, Krasnoyarsk-45 is frozen, silent, and cut off. Perfect for hiding something.

### The Jian of Still Waters

**History.** The jian arrived on Earth approximately 1,200 years ago, carried by an immortal cultivator from the Three Realms who had attained a level of power that allowed inapposite transversal — full physical transport across the universal boundary. The cultivator died on Earth. The reason is unknown. The jian remained.

It is a straight, double-edged Chinese sword of elegant construction, with a blade that shows no corrosion despite its age. Its name — Still Waters — comes from the appearance of the metal: dark and depthless, like a mountain lake at midnight. When razmerit flows through it, faint characters in seal script shimmer along the blade: the remnant of a cultivation technique imprinted by its original wielder.

**Discovery.** The jian was recovered by a Russian deep-sea expedition in the Sea of Okhotsk, where it had lain in a submerged temple structure of unknown origin. It changed hands through artifact traffickers before being acquired by the Russian Federation and assigned to the ARD.

**Properties.** The jian is a Three Realms attunement source of exceptional purity. Anyone holding or near it (within roughly the same room) gains Three Realms attunement at d8 strength. It holds 25 razmerit in its own pool. Its SFX "Law Bridge" can temporarily boost attunement strength, enabling full cultivation trait access in a non-Three Realms environment — for a cost.

The jian is also a weapon. In the Three Realms, its cutting power is d12. On Earth, without qi, it is merely an extraordinarily well-balanced sword.

### Dr. Mikhail Antonov

Antonov is a mathematician, not a physicist — his field is multidimensional harmonics, the study of how waveforms behave across spaces with more than three dimensions. It is a niche, theoretical discipline with few practical applications. Until the jian.

Attached to the ARD as a civilian consultant, Antonov was given access to the jian as part of a broad-spectrum analysis effort. Where other researchers ran spectrometry and metallurgy, Antonov ran the waveform data through his harmonic models. What he found terrified him.

The jian is a **resonator**. It hums at frequencies that don't exist in three-dimensional space. Antonov's models showed that the jian is attuned to a coherent extra-dimensional signature — a specific universe, stable and reachable. More alarmingly, his models showed that with enough power and the right resonance, the jian could be used not just to observe that universe, but to **open a door**.

Antonov knows the Russian Federation well enough to fear what it would do with a door to another world. He also knows that his research is being monitored. He cannot destroy the jian. He cannot steal it. What he can do is reach out.

His missive — encrypted, routed through academic networks, bouncing through dead-drop servers — finds the players. It contains his location, a brief explanation of what he's found, and a warning: *"They will use it. I cannot stop them. Please help me and my family leave. I will tell you everything."*

> [!note] Antonov's Family
> Antonov's wife, Dr. Elena Antonova, is a geologist who met him at university. She knows his work and has helped him hide his communications. Their daughter, Katya (14), is unaware of the situation. The extraction must include all three.

## The Three Realms

The Three Realms is a xianxia universe — a world governed by dao, qi, and the pursuit of immortality through cultivation. Its physics support meridians, spiritual energy, alchemy, sword flight, tribulation lightning, and the cyclical rise and fall of sects and dynasties across geological timescales.

### The Valley of Ascension

The Valley is the arrival point for all consciousness transversals that use the jian as an anchor. It is a place of extraordinary qi density, a natural cultivation paradise that has drawn practitioners for tens of thousands of years. The Valley is a formation laid down by an ancient array master. The formation suppresses cultivation to the foundation building realm. Cultivators coming in from outside will feel their realm suppressed and their memories fading quickly. The Valley contains:

- **The Arrival Platform**: An ancient stone dais carved with formation arrays. Consciousness transversals materialize here, given form by the Valley's ambient qi. New arrivals find themselves in cultivator bodies — baseline, untrained, but alive.
- **The Remnant Formations**: Ancient arrays left by the immortal who first carved the Valley. Some are protective. Some are traps. Some are trials — designed to test and strengthen those who pass through them. The players will encounter these as their cultivation advances.
- **The Transversal Artifacts**: Scattered through the Valley and its surrounding regions are a handful of artifacts attuned to different universes. Each is a key — a way out of the Three Realms to another world. Finding them, attuning to them, and activating them is the players' long-term objective. The jian is one such artifact, but it's on Earth. The others are here.

### Cultivation in the Three Realms

The xianxia genre follows a well-established progression structure. Transversal maps this to mechanical growth:

| Cultivation Stage | Mechanical Effect | Typical Timescale |
|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Qi Condensation** | Baseline cultivation traits (d4–d6), first SFX unlocked | Months to years |
| **Foundation Establishment** | Traits step up (d6–d8), second trait set opens | Decades |
| **Core Formation** | Traits step up (d8–d10), inapposite transversal becomes possible | Centuries |
| **Nascent Soul** | Traits step up (d10–d12), law-bridging without artifacts | Centuries to millennia |
| **Immortal Ascension** | D12+ capabilities, universe creation hinted at | Millennia |

The players begin at Qi Condensation. The Valley's accelerated qi density compresses the early stages, but true power takes time — decades, centuries. The narrative uses time-jumps and training arcs, true to the genre.

> [!tip] Trait Growth as Cultivation
> Stepping up a die *is* a cultivation breakthrough. The mechanics and the fiction are the same event described two ways. When a player's qi_reserves goes from d6 to d8, they have spent years cycling qi, broken through a bottleneck, and emerged stronger. The SFX they unlock is the technique they developed in the process.

### The Immortal Cultivator

The jian's original wielder was a cultivator who reached a level of power that allowed inapposite transversal — physical travel between universes without external technology. They came to Earth for reasons lost to history. They died on Earth — their body, if it still exists, has not been found.

In the Three Realms, this cultivator is remembered as **the Still Water Immortal**, a figure of legend. Sects have fought wars over fragments of their techniques. The Valley of Ascension is believed to be one of their created spaces — a training ground, or perhaps a prison, or perhaps a gift to those who would follow.

The players, arriving through the Still Water Immortal's jian, are assumed by the Verdant Steps Sect to be the immortal's chosen successors. This gives them protection (the sect shelters them) and danger (other sects, learning of their arrival, may want what they carry).

## Transversal Physics

### What Is Transversal?

Transversal is movement across the universal boundary — the membrane that separates one universe's laws from another's. Razmerit is the only known substance that can pierce this membrane, because razmerit is itself a piece of the boundary, solidified.

The common backend — the technological or metaphysical substrate that enables traversal — operates on razmerit resonance. An entity with razmerit attuned to a target universe can "tune" to that universe's frequency and cross.

### Consciousness Transversal

The cheapest form. The entity's consciousness — their information pattern, their self — crosses the boundary. A new body is formed in the target universe from available material (qi, in the Three Realms; physical matter on Earth; etc.). The original body remains behind, inert but alive.

This is what the jian does. When the players approach it in Antonov's lab, the jian resonates, their consciousness patterns align with the Three Realms frequency, and they vanish from Earth — leaving their bodies behind.

### Inapposite Transversal

Full physical transport. The entity, their body, and whatever they're carrying cross the boundary intact. This requires vastly more razmerit, precise attunement, and typically a constructed device (a portal, a continua device) rather than a natural artifact.

Inapposite transversal is late-game technology. The players may discover or build such devices in the Three Realms, or find that cultivation at Core Formation stage and above naturally enables it.

### Time Dilation

Different universes experience time at different rates. The ratio is a property of the universe pair, determined by the "distance" between them in dimensional space.

| Pair | Ratio | Earth Equivalent |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| Three Realms : Earth | ~1,000,000:1 | 1,000 years = ~8.8 hours |

This means:
- The players can spend centuries cultivating without the Earth timeline advancing meaningfully
- When they return to Earth, the extraction mission is still live
- Antonov, his family, and the Russian agent are exactly where they were
- The players, however, are not the same people who left

Time dilation is not uniform across all pairs. A universe "closer" to Earth in dimensional space might have a 10:1 ratio. A universe impossibly distant might have a billion-to-one ratio — or run in reverse.

### The Common Backend

Razmerit is a material — crystalline, boundary-born, native to the membranes between universes. It can be refined into composites, woven into alloys, grown into circuits. Across the multiverse, civilizations have independently discovered razmerit and built technologies around it.

The **common backend** is the aggregate of all razmerit-based technology across all universes. It is not a single device or network — it is a natural layer of reality that intelligent beings learn to access. On Earth, Antonov's harmonic models are the first mathematical description of this layer. The jian is a physical interface to it. Other universes have their own interfaces: portals, continua devices, cultivation formations, attunement chambers.

The "applications" that players use are the interfaces their characters access — the jian, a portal gun, a cultivation technique, a data-slate tuned to razmerit frequencies. The UI the player sees is the character's view through that interface. The screen is the portal.

> [!quote] The Multiverse Is the Backdrop
> Razmerit is the connecting factor. Every universe, every civilization, every artifact — they all touch the same hyper-dimensional material. The common backend is not something built; it is something discovered, over and over, by every species that looks hard enough at the spaces between spaces.

## Player Group Origins

The game supports multiple origin stories for player groups. The GM selects or the players choose. All lead to the same starting situation: arriving in Krasnoyarsk-45 to find Antonov.

### Option A: The Foundation

A private foundation focused on anomalous physics research — think the Wellcome Trust meets the Perimeter Institute. Antonov submitted a grant proposal years ago that was flagged by a senior researcher who recognized the implications. When his missive arrived, the foundation moved. They hired a small, capable team — the players — and arranged covert transport into Russia.

**Tone**: Academic espionage. Resources exist but are limited. The foundation can provide intel, funding, and extraction support — but cannot protect the players if things go wrong.

### Option B: The Consortium

A multinational technology company with a sideline in fringe-science acquisition. Antonov's former PhD student now works there as a senior engineer. When Antonov reached out through academic back-channels, the student took it to their CEO. The company deployed a corporate security team — the players — under the cover of a geological survey.

**Tone**: Corporate gray-zone. Better resources, but the company has its own agenda. They want the jian, not just Antonov. The players may face pressure to bring back more than they were sent for.

### Option C: The Old Network

No institution. Antonov reached people he trusted — former colleagues, students, friends of friends. The players are those people. They arranged their own transport, their own covers, their own plan. They're in Russia because Antonov asked, and they answered.

**Tone**: Personal stakes. No backup, no extraction, no resources beyond what the players brought themselves. Maximum freedom, maximum risk.

### Option D: The Rival State

A foreign intelligence service — not Russian, not American necessarily, but a state with the capacity to monitor Russian scientific communications. They intercepted Antonov's academic traffic, recognized the significance, and deployed a deniable team. The players are contractors, not agents — deniable if captured.

**Tone**: Spy thriller. Professional resources, but the players have a handler who may have conflicting priorities. The mission is extraction, but the agency wants the jian too.

### Custom Origins

Groups can define their own origin. The only constraints are:
1. The players arrive in Krasnoyarsk-45 intending to find Antonov
2. They don't know what the jian does (only Antonov knows that)
3. They are capable enough to handle a covert extraction in hostile territory

## Session One: The Extraction

This is the first session players experience. It establishes Earth, introduces Antonov, and triggers the transversal to the Three Realms.

### Act One: Arrival

The players arrive in Krasnoyarsk-45. It's winter. The town is small, frozen, and watched. An FSB agent, posing as a municipal administrator, takes note of strangers. The players must establish cover, avoid attention, and locate the ARD facility in the mountains above town.

**Key scenes:**
- Securing transport from the nearest city (Krasnoyarsk proper) to the closed town
- Passing the checkpoint (the town is technically closed — they need cover identities)
- The hotel — run by an elderly couple who remember when the facility was active
- The bar — locals who might talk, or might report strangers to the agent
- Reconnaissance of the mountain road

### Act Two: The Facility

The ARD facility is an aging Soviet-era research complex built into the mountainside. Security is light — the Russian Federation doesn't fully understand what they have. The players infiltrate, locate Antonov's lab, and find him.

Antonov is in a wheelchair — a childhood polio complication. He sits near a humming machine, an array of sensors and harmonic resonators surrounding a central pedestal. On the pedestal, suspended in a magnetic field, the jian spins slowly.

Antonov explains:
- What the jian is (a bridge to another universe)
- Why he reached out (the Russian Federation will weaponize it)
- What he needs (extraction for himself and his family)
- What he's discovered (the jian is active — it's been resonating more strongly in recent weeks, as if something on the other side is calling)

The players have a choice: extract Antonov immediately, or examine the jian further. If they examine it, Antonov warns them not to get too close — but the jian has other ideas.

### Act Three: Transversal

When the players approach the jian, it activates. The resonance aligns. Their consciousness patterns synchronize with the Three Realms frequency. They lose consciousness on Earth.

They awaken on the Arrival Platform in the Valley of Ascension. They are in new bodies — cultivator vessels, baseline and untrained. The Valley stretches before them: mist-shrouded peaks, ancient stone formations humming with qi, the distant roofs of the Verdant Steps Sect.

Elder Yun is there. He has seen this before. He offers tea.

Thus begins the true story of Transversal.
