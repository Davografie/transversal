# Getting Started

## What you need

- A modern browser (desktop or tablet). Transversal is a PWA — installable and touch-friendly.
- The app's URL and an active session to join.

## Log in

1. Open the app.
2. Type your name in the login field — matching players appear as you type.
3. Pick your name, or choose **New transverser** to create a fresh player.

Your player identity is stored server-side and recovered by name, so you can rejoin from any device.

## Roles

There are two roles:

- **Player** — sees a minimal UI: character sheet, dice roller, and the location.
- **Architect** — additionally sees the [[GM Panel Development|GM Panel]] and location-management tools.

Your role is stored server-side and synced when you log in.

## Claim a character

After logging in, claim the entity you'll play. An entity has one **projection per universe** — a separate body and trait set for each world it can reach (see [[Projections and Transversal]]).

## Arrive at your first location

Locations render as a full-screen image with interactive **nav dots** — zones, occupants, and destinations. Tap a dot to inspect or travel to it (see [[Location Viewer]]).

## Roll your first pool

1. Open your character sheet.
2. Tap traits to add their dice to your pool.
3. Roll:
   - **1–2 dice (simplified)** — take the highest and read an outcome tier (Fail → Marginal → Success → Exceptional → Perfect).
   - **3+ dice (dice pool)** — keep two dice for your total, set aside one as your effect die.

See [[Core Mechanics]] for pool assembly and [[Dice Mechanics]] for resolution.
